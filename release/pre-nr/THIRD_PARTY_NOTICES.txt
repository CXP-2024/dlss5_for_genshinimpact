RenoDX
------

Project: RenoDX by clshortfuse
Source: https://github.com/clshortfuse/renodx

The HDR model-picture composition used by this add-on is derived from RenoDX's
DLSS 5 Neural Rendering add-on. In particular, this includes the two-branch
luminance-ratio composition, the OkLab hue correction applied to the rescaled
model result, and the final blend between a luminance-only result and the
model's colour. The implementation here has been adapted to this add-on's
resource and exposure contracts.

The OkLab conversion matrices are Bjorn Ottosson's published constants. The
AP1 gamut matrices and sRGB transfer functions are standard colour science.
